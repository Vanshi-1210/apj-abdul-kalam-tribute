Known for his simplicity, wisdom, and dedication to teaching and science.

ABOUT DR.APJ ABDUL KALAM

Who he was-Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in strengthening India’s space and missile development programs. Despite his great achievements, he was known for his simplicity and humility.

Connection with students-Dr. Kalam had a deep connection with students and young minds. He believed that education, curiosity, and hard work could transform a nation. He spent most of his life teaching, motivating, and guiding students across India.

Why he is remembered-He is remembered not only for his scientific contributions but also for his vision of a developed and self-reliant India. His life continues to inspire millions to dream big and work honestly towards their goals.
